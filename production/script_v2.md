## How to record

- Read the **You say** column top to bottom, **one numbered line at a time, with a one-second pause between lines.** The pauses are what let me place each line on its shot.
- Fluffed a line? Pause, then redo the **whole line**. I keep the last clean take, same as v1.
- Rows marked *(nat sound)* have no VO. Just skip to the next line.
- *(optional)* lines are yours to keep or skip. If you'd say it differently, say it your way and keep the meaning.
- One long file is fine. If you record in parts, number the files in order (01, 02, ...).
