**Goal:** make people need to watch the YouTube video. Ask the questions and show the wow moments, but never give an answer. Every answer lives in the long video.
**Length:** about 45 s. For a hype reel, shorter beats 90 s: completion rate drives Reels and TikTok reach, and a 45 s teaser that people watch twice does better than a 90 s one they swipe away from. (It can be stretched to 60 s by adding more question beats, see the end.)
**Voice:** only lines you already recorded, so there's nothing new to record. **Pictures:** every shot is a real clip from your footage (file names in brackets); friend clips get the "filmed by a friend" tag. The recorded line is quoted exactly, and the cut ends where the quote ends.
**Pacing:** music-driven, a cut every 0.5–1 s, hard cuts on the beat, and a full stop (silence) before the ending.
**Captions:** burned in, big, 1–3 words at a time.

- No answers: nothing about visa-free days, Alipay, "very spicy", "yes it's safe", or "definitely worth it".
