# Day-by-day reels: scripts v1 (for review)

**Series:** "Chongqing in 6 days". One reel per day, each sending people to the full video on YouTube.
**Format:** 9:16, about 50–65 s each, fast cuts (about 0.6–1.2 s) on the music's beat, burned-in captions.

**Voice:** only takes you already recorded, each used whole. No line starts or ends in the middle of a take. Before rendering, I'll cut every line from the raw recording at a real pause and check it by transcript.

**Pictures:** every shot is a real clip, named by file. **P** means it was filmed portrait (fills the screen). **L** means landscape, centre-cropped; I only use these where the moment has no portrait version.

**Every reel:**
- **Opens with** a small tag "CHONGQING · DAY X/6".
- **Ends with** a 3 s card: "WATCH THE FULL VIDEO" with your thumbnail, "▶ YouTube · Shawnlemuel" and "link in bio".

**Music:** I'll send you a preview to approve after the scripts. My suggestion is the same upbeat track as the YouTube video, so the series sounds like one show.

Lines marked *(optional)* can come out to make a reel shorter.
