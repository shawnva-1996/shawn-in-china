## Titles

Scored with vidIQ's title scorer (long-form, 0–100) on 22 Oct 2026. All four score high, so the choice comes down to which promise the video pays off best.

| # | Title | vidIQ | Promise it makes |

| T1 | **Chongqing Isn't Real… So I Went Inside** | 96 | The city looks fake (drone show, 8D streets), and Shawn got access. **Recommended.** |
