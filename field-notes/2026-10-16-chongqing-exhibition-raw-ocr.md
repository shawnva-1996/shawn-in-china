Chongqing Lianglu Guoyuan Port Comprehensive Bonded Zone was formerly Chongqing Lianglu Cuntan Bonded Port Area, which was approved by the State Council in November 2008. It was renamed Lianglu Guoyuan Port Comprehensive Bonded Zone in January 2022. The zone consists of three major functional sections:
Airport Economic Demonstration Zone, Guoyuan Port National Logistics Hub Port-side Economic Zone and Cuntan International New Town Bonded Economic Zone.
