# Itinerary Booklet: OCR Transcription

- **Source:** Five photos of the printed "Appendix: Itinerary" pages from the mission journal booklet. They are stored in Google Drive under TAL/Itinerary as IMG_5850.HEIC to IMG_5854.HEIC and were photographed on 13 Oct 2026.
- **Method:** The HEIC files were converted to JPG and read with vision OCR on 2026-10-21. The text was checked line by line against the photos.
- **Handling note:** This copy keeps the wording, times and capitalisation as printed. Faded or foil-printed characters that the camera blurred are marked `[?]` where the reading is uncertain. In the original layout, Program A and Program B run side by side. Here they are written one after the other.
