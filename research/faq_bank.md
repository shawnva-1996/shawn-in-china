## B. Getting around the "8D city"

| # | Question | Brief answer | Status and source | Where it fits in the footage |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | Why is Chongqing called the 8D or cyberpunk city? | It is built on mountains where two rivers meet (the Yangtze and the Jialing), so roads, rail and buildings are stacked. A street can enter a building on its 22nd floor. At night the neon skyline looks like science fiction. | Verified as common reporting (the reference videos and DW). | Jiefangbei 16 Oct night, Chaotianmen 15 Oct night |
| B2 | Is Google Maps useless there? | Yes, partly because the layers confuse GPS, and Google is blocked anyway. Use a Chinese map app and check *which level* your destination is on. | DW and reference videos. Shawn to confirm a personal "lost" moment. | Any walking clip |
| B3 | Is it easy to get around? | The metro and monorail are cheap and extensive (the Liziba "train through a building" is a monorail station). Ride-hailing is cheap. Expect a lot of stairs. | Reference videos. Shawn to confirm. | Transit clips, if Gemini finds them |
| B4 | How walkable is it? | Very, but vertical. Wear good shoes, and plan routes downhill where you can (Shancheng Alley is easiest walked top to bottom). | Around The World We Go. | 17 Oct exploring |
